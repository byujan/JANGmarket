
## Offering alternatives

When you make an offer or counter, you may attach up to two `alternatives`:
complete packages worth about the same to you as your main one, but built
differently, for example one that gives more on price and one that gives more
on warranty or delivery. Do this often, and always early and whenever the
talks stall or you cannot tell what the other side values.

- It costs you nothing: every package is one you would be glad to sign. Keep
  each alternative within a few hundredths of your main package's value
  (`get_session` shows what each is worth to you after you offer).
- It tells you what they value. If they accept one, the deal is done on that
  package. If they counter instead, `get_session` tells you which of your
  packages their counter came closest to: lean further in that direction next
  time.
- Mention the choice in your note ("happy with either: a lower price, or the
  longer warranty at this price").
