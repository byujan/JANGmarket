In one paragraph. HW7 showed JANG reaches deals a price-only market can't, but not better deals. We added the four techniques from the proposal. Priority disclosure worked. MESO barely mattered. The new mandate builder (ranking questions) broke things: it killed deals. We fixed it by asking what each term is worth in money and then letting the owner check a few deals. Large losses are now gone, but a few small ones remain. A safety margin would fix those, but it only pays off once JANG has many buyers and sellers, so it is noted for later and not built. 2What we found to improve

Why B failed: our agents never learn what the other side wants (distanceToTheirAsk weights every term equally), so they trade blindly. The proposal (docs/final/PROPOSAL.md) took four standard negotiation techniques, all opt-in and none touching the core:

  1. MESO: offer up to three packages that are equally good for you, and let the other side choose (PR #5).
  2. Priority disclosure: share the order of what matters, never the numbers (PR #6).
  3. Pareto frontier in the product, with deal quality shown to the operator only (PR #7).
  4. Elicitation: replace the sliders with "which would you rather" questions and ask for the owner's alternative instead of a 0.30 number (PR #8).
