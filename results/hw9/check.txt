1. The market
─────────────────────────────────────
Clearance 65-inch OLED television, last season's model (late 2026)
A retailer is clearing last season's 65-inch OLED panels ahead of Black Friday, after the new models launched in spring. Street prices for this class sit around 1,100 to 1,350 USD. Price is not the only lever: delivery speed, total warranty length (12 months from the manufacturer, extendable by the retailer) and a bundled soundbar are all on the table.

Negotiable terms: Price, Delivery, Warranty, Soundbar bundled

Private to the buyer's owner: You shop for a household that has watched this TV's price for months and knows it has sold for around 1,100 to 1,250 USD on sale. You will not pay more than 1,300 USD and would love to get it under 1,000. You care most about price, then about a longer warranty, because a repair on a 65-inch OLED is expensive. A soundbar is a nice extra but not worth much to you, and you can wait a few weeks for delivery.
Private to the seller's owner: You clear inventory for an electronics retailer. After vendor support, each panel cost you about 1,050 USD and you may not sell below that. The new models are already on the floor, so you want these gone before Black Friday. Extending the warranty is cheap for you, because few panels fail in their second or third year, so you would much rather add warranty than cut the price. Bundling a soundbar costs you real money, and expedited delivery costs a little more than standard delivery.

Neither agent is shown the other's paragraph, limits or preferences.
